Drawing the floor plan

  • Draw walls corner to corner. Every line end must touch another line, at its end or anywhere along it.
  • Doors go in gaps: wall end, door, wall end. A door end touches exactly one wall, and nothing touches a door's middle. Drawing a door along a wall replaces that part of the wall.
  • Every enclosed area is a room: name it with the Room tool. All rooms on a floor must be reachable through doors.
  • A space that isn't part of the game (a gap left between rooms) is void: mark it with the Void tool, or press Slice void in the Inspector to mark every unnamed space without a door. A void has no doors and no tasks.
  • Correct edges (Inspector, nothing selected) turns every line to the nearest multiple of 45°, keeping corners joined.
  • Drag tasks from the Tasks tab into rooms. Tasks left off the plan are switched off when you save. Bound and repeating tasks need all their parts placed.
